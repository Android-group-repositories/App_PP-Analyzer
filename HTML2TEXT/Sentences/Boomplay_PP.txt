We collect and use metadata when you Post content (including images, text, and audio) to Boomplay.
The metadata describes other data and provides information about your content that may not always be evident to the viewer.
The metadata can describe how, when, and by whom the piece of content was collected, and how that content is formatted.
It includes information such as your account name that enables other users to trace back the content to your user account.
Metadata also consists of additional data that you choose to provide with the content, such as any hashtags used to mark keywords to the comments.
